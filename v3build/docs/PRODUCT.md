# Product blueprint

## Workspace setup
- ICP
- Offer
- Intent signals
- Countries
- Industries
- Company size
- Titles
- Technologies
- Keywords
- Channels/providers
- AI policy
- Booking link

## Autonomous pipeline
Discover companies → identify evidence → enrich decision makers → verify contactability → deduplicate → suppress → score → personalize → send/call → classify response → qualify → book → stop.

## AI SDR behavior
- Research from evidence
- Explain why-now
- Ask one question at a time
- Handle objections from configured offer knowledge
- Never invent facts
- Stop on opt-out
- Escalate sensitive commitments

## International voice
Provider abstraction supports Twilio, Exotel and SIP-style adapters. Country, caller ID, hours and consent rules must be enforced by the production adapter/policy layer.
