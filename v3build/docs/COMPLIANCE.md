# Compliance guardrails

The platform is designed to assist compliant outbound, not bypass restrictions.

1. Contact details must come from legitimate/authorized sources.
2. Never infer or guess a person's email or phone number.
3. Maintain workspace and global suppression/DNC lists.
4. Honor unsubscribe and opt-out immediately.
5. Respect provider rate limits and terms.
6. Respect country-specific marketing, privacy, telemarketing, recording, caller-ID and calling-hour requirements.
7. Voice agents must not impersonate humans and should identify as AI where required or asked.
8. Store provenance for evidence and enrichment.
9. Keep audit events for material automated decisions.
10. Escalate sensitive negotiations and commitments to humans.
